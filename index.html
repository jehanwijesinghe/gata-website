The credible home of aviation training in Sri Lanka

GATA exists to raise the standard of aviation, cargo and dangerous goods training in Sri Lanka — and to align it with the standards the global industry already expects.

Our Story

Specialist training, built to international standards

Unlike academies that focus only on cabin crew programmes, GATA specialises in industry-certified aviation training across the full value chain — from air cargo and ULD handling to CAA-approved dangerous goods certification and airline-ready career programmes.

Our purpose is simple: to give aviation professionals and future talent qualifications that are recognised, respected and ready for the workplace.
